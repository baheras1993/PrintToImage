# PrintToImage commercial license

PrintToImage is **free** for personal and noncommercial use. Using it in a business needs a **commercial license**:
a low, one-time price per server, with no per-user or per-PC fees.

## Free: no license needed

- Personal use: at home, hobby projects, study, testing.
- Charities and non-profit organisations.
- Schools, universities and public research organisations.
- Public hospitals and public health organisations, government offices and public safety organisations.
- **Trying it at work:** you may install and test PrintToImage in a business for **30 days** before you buy.

These come from the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) that PrintToImage is published under. The 30-day trial
is an extra permission from the copyright holder.

## Commercial license needed

Any use by or for a business after the 30-day trial. For example:

- private clinics, practices and laboratories
- company offices, shops, warehouses and factories
- IT service providers who install it for their customers
- selling PrintToImage, or including it in another product or service

## What a commercial license gives you

- Use of PrintToImage in your business on **one server** (or PC), for **all the computers that print to it**, with no time limit.
- All updates of the same major version (1.x).
- Permission to change the program for your own use.

Several servers, a whole organisation, or redistribution with your own product: ask for a quote.
